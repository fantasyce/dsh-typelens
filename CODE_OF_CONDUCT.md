# Community Code of Conduct

DSH TypeLens welcomes respectful, constructive participation regardless of
experience, identity, background, or employer.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss concrete behavior and evidence.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Protect private source code, credentials, and model-provider data.
- Report security concerns through the private process in `SECURITY.md`.
- Correct mistakes transparently.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing
private data, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may
remove contributions or restrict participation when needed to protect the
community. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the
project publicly.
